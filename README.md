# Federal Republic of Kabylia

**Official GitHub organization of the Federal Republic of Kabylia**

This organization hosts the official digital infrastructure, legal documents, design system, and public technical resources of the Federal Republic of Kabylia.

---

## About

The Federal Republic of Kabylia is a self-determined state representing the Kabyle people. This GitHub organization serves as the central platform for:

- Official legal texts and institutional documentation
- National identity and visual standards
- Digital public infrastructure
- Open technical resources and standards

All repositories under this organization are maintained with the goal of transparency, sovereignty, and long-term digital resilience.

---

## Mission

We build open, accessible, and resilient digital infrastructure for a free and sovereign Kabyle nation.

Our guiding principles are:

- Sovereignty and digital independence
- Transparency and public accountability
- Open standards and interoperability
- Accessibility and multilingual inclusion
- Long-term resilience and public trust
