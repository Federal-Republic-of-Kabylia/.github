# Code of Conduct

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for all contributors and participants.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Acknowledging different viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on facts, clarity, and public value
- Offering help and feedback in a professional manner

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or abusive language
- Personal attacks or discriminatory remarks
- Unwelcome conduct toward any community member
- Publishing misleading, offensive, or harmful content

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the project maintainers or the appropriate official channel for the repository.

We take reports seriously and will respond promptly and appropriately.
