# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Federal Republic of Kabylia digital infrastructure and public resources.

## Scope

This organization hosts public documentation, standards, identity assets, and policy materials. Contributions should support transparency, accessibility, accuracy, and institutional integrity.

## How to contribute

1. Review the repository purpose and existing documentation before proposing changes.
2. Open or comment on an issue before starting major edits.
3. Keep changes focused, well-documented, and aligned with official terminology and public values.
4. Use clear commit messages and descriptive pull requests.

## Standards

- Prefer factual, evidenced, and publicly verifiable content.
- Respect multilingual context, especially Kabyle, French, and English.
- Ensure accessibility and readability for a broad public audience.
- Do not publish unofficial or misleading institutional materials as official content.

## Review process

Changes may be reviewed by maintainers for accuracy, legal consistency, design quality, and alignment with the organization’s mission.

## Questions

For general questions, use the repository issue tracker or contact the relevant project maintainers through the official organization channels.
