The Federal Republic of Kabylia is building a sovereign, transparent, and resilient digital ecosystem for the public interest. This organization hosts the core institutional resources, legal foundation, standards, identities, and public-facing assets of the State.

## Mission

We develop open, accessible, and durable digital infrastructure for a free and self-determined Kabyle nation.

Our guiding principles are:

- Sovereignty and digital independence
- Transparency and public accountability
- Open standards and interoperability
- Accessibility and multilingual inclusion
- Long-term resilience and public trust

## Institutional pillars

| Pillar | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Governance | Constitution, institutions, legal archives, parliament, and public administration structure |
| Identity | National symbols, brand assets, visual system, and official communication standards |
| Digital public infrastructure | Platforms, systems architecture, interoperability, and secure public technology |
| Standards & trust | Accessibility rules, cybersecurity policy, technical governance, and digital identity |
