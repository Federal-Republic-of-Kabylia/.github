The Federal Republic of Kabylia is building a sovereign, transparent, and resilient digital ecosystem for the public interest. This organization hosts the core institutional resources, legal foundation, standards, identities, and public-facing assets of the State.

---

## 🤝 Join the Movement: Contribute to Kabylia's Digital Ecosystem

Building a sovereign state's digital infrastructure requires open collaboration. Whether you are a developer, designer, linguist, security expert, or writer, your skills can shape the future of Kabylia’s public technology.

### How you can help

- **💻 Code & Architecture**: Help build accessible web portals, digital identity frameworks, and secure public APIs (`gov-portal`, `digital-identity`, `standards`).
- **🎨 Design & UX**: Improve our UI systems, ensure WCAG compliance, and refine visual assets (`design-system`, `brand-assets`).
- **🌐 Localization & Linguistics**: Translate interfaces and legal texts into Kabyle, English, or French, and expand institutional terminology (`kabyle-language`).
- **🔒 Security & Infrastructure**: Audit repositories, propose security enhancements, and strengthen resilience policy (`security-policy`, `digital-infrastructure`).
- **📄 Legal & Documentation**: Review constitutional texts, structure open data, and clarify administrative documents (`constitution`, `official-journal`).

### Getting started

1. **Explore the issues**: Browse our repositories and look for tags like `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
2. **Read the guidelines**: Check the `CONTRIBUTING.md` file in each repository before submitting a Pull Request.
3. **Propose changes**: Open an issue to discuss new features or submit a PR directly for bug fixes and documentation updates.

> **Note**: All contributors are expected to uphold our commitment to transparency, security, and civic respect.

## Mission

We develop open, accessible, and durable digital infrastructure for a free and self-determined Kabyle nation.

Our guiding principles are:

- Sovereignty and digital independence
- Transparency and public accountability
- Open standards and interoperability
- Accessibility and multilingual inclusion
- Long-term resilience and public trust

## Institutional pillars

| Pillar | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Governance | Constitution, institutions, legal archives, parliament, and public administration structure |
| Identity | National symbols, brand assets, visual system, and official communication standards |
| Digital public infrastructure | Platforms, systems architecture, interoperability, and secure public technology |
| Standards & trust | Accessibility rules, cybersecurity policy, technical governance, and digital identity |
